# RecItDown Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

RecItDown is a personal money tracker for Android, developed by Ezra Alvarion. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## The short version

Everything you record stays on your device. RecItDown has no accounts, no servers, no ads, and the developer adds no analytics or tracking. The developer never receives your records.

## What the app stores

- Your records (amounts, dates, accounts, categories, notes), accounts, categories, presets and budgets, in a database on your device.
- Your app settings (theme, accent colour, currency) and home-screen widget choices, on your device.

## Network access

RecItDown's own features never use the network: your records, accounts, categories and presets are not sent anywhere.

The app includes Google's Play Billing library so you can buy Pro. That library adds the Android internet and network-state permissions and may send technical diagnostics about billing to Google, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Purchases

The Pro purchase is handled by Google Play under Google's terms. The developer never sees your card or other payment details. Google gives the developer order records for sales and tax purposes, such as the order number, product, price and country. The app only stores on your device whether Pro is unlocked.

## Files you create

CSV exports and JSON backups are written only where you choose to save them, through Android's file picker. CSV imports and backup restores read only the file you pick. Once a file leaves the app, it is under your control; keep backups somewhere you trust, because they contain your financial records in plain text.

## Android system backup

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include RecItDown's data in the backup it stores in your Google account, under Google's terms. The developer has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device's settings.

## Sharing

The developer does not sell or share your information, and your records are never shared with anyone unless you export a file and share it yourself. Google handles purchases and billing diagnostics as described above.

Sending feedback opens your own email app; nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you do, the developer receives your email address and message, uses them only to reply, and you can ask for them to be deleted.

## Children

RecItDown is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Deleting your data

Use Configure › Manage Data › Erase all data in the app, or uninstall it. Either removes the data from your device. Files you exported or backed up elsewhere are not affected.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
